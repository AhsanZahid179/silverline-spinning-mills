Our Product Range

Manufactured to meet the needs of weavers, knitters, and traders.

Combed Cotton Yarn

Smooth, high-strength yarn with short fibers and impurities removed during combing, resulting in a finer, more even yarn suited for premium fabric applications.

Count: 20s–40s Fine Fabrics Knitwear

Carded Cotton Yarn

Reliable, cost-effective cotton yarn produced through standard carding, offering a good balance of strength and affordability for everyday textile production.

Count: 10s–30s General Weaving Everyday Use

Blended (Poly-Cotton) Yarn

A durable cotton-polyester blend that combines the softness of cotton with the strength and wrinkle-resistance of polyester, ideal for versatile garment production.

Count: 20s–30s Durable Garments Versatile

Open-End (OE) Yarn

Economical, coarse-count yarn produced via open-end spinning, valued for its bulk and strength in heavy-duty applications like denim and industrial fabric.

Count: 6s–20s Denim Industrial Fabric
